{\sffamily\Large Equations and code architecture\par}\vspace{5mm}
\textbf{Application author : Romain VIALA}\par
Version 1.10.0 · 3 November 2026\par

This manual describes the physical, numerical, acoustic, statistical and signal-processing equations in this distribution. Pixel layout and form validation are excluded. Illustrative parameters are not measurements.\par

References are relative to the repository root and use one-based lines. Ranges identify implementing function blocks; single lines identify code anchors. A compact JavaScript line may contain several equations. Code is authoritative for bounds and implementation details. source-map.json records anchors and SHA-256 hashes; verify\_references.py detects changes.\par

Conventions: SI unless stated otherwise; $f$ in Hz, $\omega=2\pi f$ in rad/s, sample rate $f_s$, time $t$ in seconds, $i^2=-1$. Amplitude gain $10^{g/20}$ for g in dB; $\sinc x=\sin x/x$, with $\sinc0=1$.\par

\section{Models and equations}
\begin{samepage}\Needspace{9\baselineskip}\subsection{Orthotropic tensor}
\loc{\texttt{backend/catalog.py} : 61-65}
\begin{align}\sigma&=C\varepsilon,\quad C=S^{-1},\quad S\succ0,\\
S_{ii}&=1/E_i\ (i=1,2,3),\quad (S_{44},S_{55},S_{66})=(1/G_{LR},1/G_{LT},1/G_{RT}),\\
S_{12}&=-\nu_{LR}/E_L,\quad S_{13}=-\nu_{LT}/E_L,\quad S_{23}=-\nu_{RT}/E_R,\\
S_{ij}&=S_{ji}.\end{align}

Voigt order is LL, RR, TT, LR, LT, RT with engineering shear. GPa converts to Pa by 1e9. Symmetric compliance enforces Poisson reciprocity. Nonpositive energy is rejected.\par
\end{samepage}
\begin{samepage}\Needspace{9\baselineskip}\subsection{TET10 and quadrature}
\loc{\texttt{backend/fem.py} : 17-21}
\begin{align}N_i&=\lambda_i(2\lambda_i-1),\quad N_{ij}=4\lambda_i\lambda_j\ (i<j),\quad\sum_{i=1}^4\lambda_i=1,\\
x&=\sum_{a=1}^{10}N_ax_a,\quad J=\partial x/\partial\xi,\quad\nabla_xN=J^{-T}\nabla_\xi N,\\
K_e&=\int B^TCB\,dV,\qquad M_e=\rho\int N^TN\otimes I_3\,dV.\end{align}

Four vertices and six edge midpoints. B computes engineering strain; mass is consistent. Eleven-point quadrature: centroid weight -74/5625; four permutations of (11/14,1/14,1/14,1/14) with weight 343/45000; six permutations of (a,a,b,b), where a=(1+sqrt(5/14))/4 and b=(1-sqrt(5/14))/4, with weight 28/1125. These are exact algorithm constants, not measured precision. Weights sum to 1/6.\par
\end{samepage}
\begin{samepage}\Needspace{9\baselineskip}\subsection{Elastic modes}
\loc{\texttt{backend/fem.py} : 53-99}
\begin{align}K\phi_j&=\lambda_j M\phi_j,\quad\phi_i^TM\phi_j=\delta_{ij},\\
f_j&=\sqrt{\lambda_j}/(2\pi),\qquad r_j=\frac{\|K\phi_j-\lambda_jM\phi_j\|}{\max(\|K\phi_j\|,\epsilon)}.\end{align}

Sparse shifted eigensolver, removal of constrained degrees and six free-free rigid modes. Normalization is mass-based. Low residual does not establish mesh convergence; out-of-band modes do not justify accurate extrapolation.\par
\end{samepage}
\begin{samepage}\Needspace{9\baselineskip}\subsection{Reference outlines and cutaway}
\loc{\texttt{backend/contours.py} : 16-17, 28-57, 59-77}
\begin{align}(x_j,y_j)&=(W u_j,L v_j),\\B(t)&=(1-t)^3P_0+3(1-t)^2tP_1+3(1-t)t^2P_2+t^3P_3,\\b(t)&=t^2(3-2t),\quad a\prime(t)=a(t)+(1-b)(N-a_0)+b(R-a_1).\end{align}

Normalized (u,v) points are GMT 1.6 reference data documented in CONTOURS.md. Width W and length L in metres scale the outline. These are no longer original PCHIP half-widths. Separate content conditions: PUBLICATION.md.\par

\begin{samepage}\Needspace{9\baselineskip}\subsection{Scaling and arch}

\begin{align}x'&=W[x-(x_{min}+x_{max})/2]/(x_{max}-x_{min}),\\

\begin{samepage}\Needspace{9\baselineskip}\subsection{Soundhole and apertures}
\loc{\texttt{backend/geometry.py} : 12-25, 27-36, 38-43}
\begin{align}x(\theta)&=c_x+r_x\cos\theta,\quad y(\theta)=c_y+r_y\sin\theta,\\
p_{SVG}&=(width\,u+c_x,height\,v+c_y),\\

\begin{samepage}\Needspace{9\baselineskip}\subsection{SVG transforms and curves}
\loc{\texttt{backend/svg\_holes.py} : 147-189, 27-43, 45-55, 57-76}
\begin{align}\widetilde p'&=T_1T_2\cdots T_n\widetilde p,\quad\widetilde p=(x,y,1)^T,\\
R(\phi)&=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\phi&-\sin\phi\\\sin\phi&\cos\phi\end{pmatrix},\quad p'=R(p-c)+c,\\
p_{ij}^{new}&=(p_i+p_{i+1})/2,\\
p(\theta)&=c+R(\phi)(r_x\cos\theta,r_y\sin\theta)^T,\\

Affine matrices, rotations, translation, scale and skew compose in SVG order. Bézier uses de Casteljau subdivision until chord-distance and control-polygon excess meet tolerance, depth at most 16. Elliptic arcs use SVG endpoint-to-center conversion and sagitta-bounded step, at most 4096 points. Only inert geometry is read, with no script or external-link execution.\par
\end{samepage}
\begin{samepage}\Needspace{9\baselineskip}\subsection{Local frames and losses}
\loc{\texttt{backend/fem.py} : 23-44, 46-51, 53-99}
\begin{align}\varepsilon_{LRT}&=R^T\varepsilon R,\quad e_{LRT}=Qe,\quad C_{global}=Q^TCQ,\\
K_{loss}&=\sum_p\eta_p K_p,\quad\eta_j=\phi_j^TK_{loss}\phi_j/\omega_j^2,\quad\zeta_j=\eta_j/2.\end{align}

Material frame follows each component, grain/ring angles and arch, with orthonormalization. Loss matrix sums component stiffnesses weighted by loss; modal damping projects onto mass-normalized modes.\par

\begin{samepage}\Needspace{9\baselineskip}\subsection{Mechanical ports and surfaces}

Twelve ports represent six strings in normal and tangential directions. Interpolation and force spreading use the same quadratic functions. Exact quadratic triangle area averaging gives zero vertex weights and one third at edge midpoints, yielding radiating panel volume displacement.\par

\begin{samepage}\Needspace{9\baselineskip}\subsection{Internal TET4 acoustics}

\begin{samepage}\Needspace{9\baselineskip}\subsection{Static residual modes}
\loc{\texttt{backend/reduction.py} : 11-46}
\begin{align}K X&=B,\quad R=X-\Phi\Omega^{-2}\Phi^TB,\\
R&\leftarrow R-\Phi\Phi^TMR,\quad R^TKR=U\Lambda U^T,\quad Q=RU\Lambda^{-1/2},\\
Q^TKQz&=\lambda Q^TMQz,\\
\epsilon_{stat}&=\frac{\|P^T\Omega^{-2}P+P_r^T\Lambda_r^{-1}P_r-B^TX\|}{\|B^TX\|}.\end{align}

Free-free static solve uses a saddle system with six mass-orthogonal rigid motions. Orthogonalization repeats twice and small energy eigenvalues are removed. Residual basis completes port response but does not make the model exact at arbitrarily high frequency.\par
\end{samepage}
\begin{samepage}\Needspace{9\baselineskip}\subsection{Aperture and Helmholtz}
\loc{\texttt{backend/response.py} : 35-68, 82-91}
\begin{align}r_{eq}&=\sqrt{S/\pi},\quad\ell_{end}=a_{end}r_{eq},\quad M_h=\rho_a\ell_{end}/S,\\
R_h&=r_{factor}\left[\frac{\rho_a\omega^2}{4\pi c}+0.05M_h\max\left(1,c\sqrt{S/(V\ell_{end})}\right)\right],\\
d_h&=-\omega^2M_h+i\omega R_h,\\
f_H^{display}&=\frac{c}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac S{V(t_{top}+a_{end}r_{eq})}}.\end{align}

The solver uses aperture mass based on end correction and approximate resistance. The displayed Helmholtz guide adds top thickness and assumes a rigid cavity; it does not replace the coupled solution. These two effective lengths must not be conflated.\par
\end{samepage}
\begin{samepage}\Needspace{9\baselineskip}\subsection{Vibroacoustic solve}
\loc{\texttt{backend/response.py} : 25-33, 70-80}
\begin{align}d_j&=\omega_j^2-\omega^2+2i\omega\zeta_j\omega_j,\\
d_a&=k_a^2-(\omega/c)^2+2i\zeta_a\omega\omega_a/c^2,\quad\zeta_a=0.025,\\
D&=\diag(d_j,d_h),\quad A=\begin{bmatrix}C\\h\end{bmatrix},\quad q_0=D^{-1}F,\\
W&=\diag(d_a/(\rho_a\omega^2))-A^TD^{-1}A,\\
a&=W^{-1}A^Tq_0,\quad q=q_0+D^{-1}Aa,\quad Y=i\omega P^Tq.\end{align}

This Schur complement eliminates mechanical variables in reduced space, then recovers structure and aperture coordinates. Peak edits multiply both emitting and receiving projections by 10\textasciicircum{}(gain/40). DC is treated separately.\par
\end{samepage}
\begin{samepage}\Needspace{9\baselineskip}\subsection{Exterior radiation}

\begin{align}G(d,\omega)&=e^{-i\omega d/c}/d,\\
H_{pF}&=-\frac{\rho_a\omega^2}{4\pi}\left[\sum_sG(d_s,\omega)S_s q_s+G(d_h,\omega)q_h\right].\end{align}

Monopole-panel sum propagates toward the listening point. Distance is bounded at 3 cm. Exterior radiation is approximate, not full BEM with radiation-loading feedback. Simulated pressure is not microphone calibration.\par

\begin{samepage}\Needspace{9\baselineskip}\subsection{Corde : String tuning and loss}

\begin{samepage}\Needspace{9\baselineskip}\subsection{Stiff-string impedance}
\loc{\texttt{backend/strings.py} : 15-20}
\begin{align}\omega_c&=\omega-i\alpha,\quad\Delta=\sqrt{T^2+4EI\mu\omega_c^2},\\
k^2&=\frac{2\mu\omega_c^2}{T+\Delta},\quad\kappa^2=\frac{T+\Delta}{2EI},\quad A=\frac{\kappa^2}{\kappa^2+k^2},\quad B=1-A,\\
D_s&=(T+EI k^2)k\cot(kL)A-EI k^2\kappa\coth(\kappa L)B,\\
Z_s&=D_s/(i\omega).\end{align}

The k² form avoids ill-conditioned subtraction. Negligible EI gives D=T k cot(kL), k=omega\_c sqrt(mu/T). Code sets Z=0 at DC. This passive impedance represents bridge string loading.\par
\end{samepage}

\begin{samepage}\Needspace{9\baselineskip}\subsection{Blocked force and synthesis}
\loc{\texttt{backend/audio.py} : 27-67, 69-75, 8-12}
